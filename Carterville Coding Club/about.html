Carterville High School

For students, by students.

A place to learn, experiment, build, and make cool tech together.

Ideas deserve somewhere to go.

We believe software is best learned by creating it. Coding isn't just about learning a language or writing lines of code, it's about curiosity, experimentation, problem-solving, and turning ideas into something real.

Carterville Coding Club gives students the space to explore, build, and learn together. Whether you're creating a game, designing a website, or making a tool to help others, every project is an opportunity to learn something new. We don't expect students to know everything before they begin. We encourage students to start with curiosity, learn through us, and grow.

We empower students to turn ideas into games, apps, and technology that inspire creativity, solve problems, and make an impact.
